01 / OVERVIEW

From finding answers
to completing tasks.

Knowledge assistants, predictable workflows and adaptive agents need different designs. Start with the business objective and choose the appropriate complexity.

01

Knowledge assistants

Retrieve relevant evidence and return sourced answers. Identify missing information when evidence is insufficient.

02

Business workflows

Connect models, rules and business interfaces through defined steps for repeatable tasks with clear acceptance criteria.

03

Task-oriented agents

Select tools, inspect results and adapt a plan within permission boundaries, budgets and stopping conditions.

02 / RETRIEVAL AUGMENTED GENERATION

Ground answers in evidence.

RAG retrieves enterprise information before generation. Data freshness, retrieval relevance and citation accuracy all contribute to trustworthy responses.

01 →

Ingest & prepare

Parse documents and tables; preserve sources, versions, timestamps and access metadata.

02 →

Index & refresh

Chunk by structure, build text and vector indexes, and synchronize additions, changes and deletions.

03 →

Retrieve & rerank

Combine keyword and semantic search within access permissions, then prioritize relevant evidence.

04 →

Generate & cite

Assemble context and link answers to source passages. A citation alone does not guarantee correctness.

05 →

Evaluate & improve

Test retrieval coverage, factual support and citations on representative questions; improve data and configuration.

For complex questions, agentic RAG can decompose queries, retrieve iteratively and check evidence coverage. Validate whether the quality gain justifies extra latency and cost.

03 / AGENTIC EXECUTION

Close the execution loop.

Use an agent when a task needs dynamic decisions about the next step. Prefer predictable workflows when the sequence is already known.

01

Goals & planning

Define inputs, success criteria, step limits and budgets before execution.

02

Tools & permissions

Define clear interfaces for retrieval, database queries and business operations; scope access to the user.

03

State & recovery

Track progress, tool outputs and checkpoints; design timeouts, retries, idempotency and recovery.

04

Action confirmation

Introduce review points for sending, writing and other consequential actions, with clear targets and impact.

05

Results & verification

Check outputs and actual system state against business rules; retry, recover or hand over when needed.

06

Tracing & evaluation

Connect model calls and tool events to task outcomes; test the impact of version changes.

FAQ

Plan your first deployment.

Does RAG eliminate hallucinations?

No. Missing evidence, conflicting documents and misinterpretation can still produce errors. Verify evidence and citations, and define when to abstain.

How does RAG differ from fine-tuning?

RAG supplies private or changing knowledge at request time. Fine-tuning primarily changes behavior, style or task performance. They can be combined.

Should we start with multiple agents?

No. Validate simple retrieval or a single-agent workflow first. Add coordination only when task decomposition delivers a measured benefit.

Can we validate in our own environment?

A validation plan can be scoped around local data boundaries, subject to model licensing, hardware, software adaptation and business interfaces. Share sample data and deployment constraints.
