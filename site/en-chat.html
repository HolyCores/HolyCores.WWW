CONTEXT → REASONING → RESULT

Better answers begin with better context.

Describe the goal, background, and constraints. Add information as you iterate, and apply human judgment to important conclusions.
  1. 01

    State the objective

    Describe what you want to understand, create, or complete.

  2. 02

    Add context

    Provide audience, constraints, source material, and success criteria.

  3. 03

    Iterate together

    Use follow-ups, revisions, and comparisons to refine the result.

  4. 04

    Review and use

    Verify important information and keep final decisions with the user.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Intelligent assistance, with human judgment.

High-risk, professional, or time-sensitive information should be checked against authoritative sources, operating rules, and human review.
PRIVACY

Handle sensitive data carefully

Do not submit confidential, personal, or regulated data without proper authorization.

VERIFY

Verify critical facts

Model output can be wrong. Important conclusions require independent verification.

HUMAN CONTROL

Keep people in control

Use the platform to support thinking and work, not to replace accountable decision makers.
