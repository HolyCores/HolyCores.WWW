About the role

Define next-generation AI compute and RISC-V architecture by translating workloads into decisions across compute, memory, interconnect, power, and software programmability.

What you will do

  • Analyze representative AI and systems workloads and build performance models
  • Define microarchitecture, dataflow, memory hierarchy, interconnect, or power goals
  • Evaluate tradeoffs with RTL, compiler, kernel, systems, and verification teams
  • Calibrate architectural assumptions with models, prototypes, and silicon data

What we look for

  • Background in computer architecture, electrical engineering, or a related field
  • Understanding of CPU, NPU, GPU, or high-performance interconnect design
  • Experience in performance modeling, workload analysis, or microarchitecture
  • Ability to communicate complex tradeoffs and drive cross-layer decisions

How we work

We value first-principles reasoning, direct communication, measurable engineering evidence, and collaboration across silicon, systems, software, and customer workloads.
