CAREERS / OPEN ROLE

AI/ML Physical Design Flow Engineer

Shanghai, ChinaApply now →

TEAM

HOLYCORES

Full-time · Shanghai

About the role

Own critical engineering work that turns advanced AI silicon constraints into a manufacturable design while balancing performance, power, area, reliability, and production risk.

What you will do

  • Build and maintain implementation, analysis, or packaging flows for advanced process technology
  • Partner with architecture, RTL, verification, DFT, packaging, and manufacturing teams
  • Diagnose timing, power, signal-integrity, thermal, or yield issues and drive closure
  • Improve flow quality and repeatability through automation, metrics, and reviews

What we look for

  • Background in electrical engineering, microelectronics, computer engineering, or a related field
  • Depth in at least one semiconductor implementation, signoff, or advanced packaging discipline
  • Ability to improve engineering workflows with scripting and data analysis
  • Strong cross-functional communication and end-to-end ownership

How we work

We value first-principles reasoning, direct communication, measurable engineering evidence, and collaboration across silicon, systems, software, and customer workloads.
